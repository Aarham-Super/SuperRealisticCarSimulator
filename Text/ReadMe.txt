SuperRealisticCarSimulator 2026

Welcome to SuperRealisticCarSimulator 2026, the ultimate PC driving simulator. This game features realistic car physics, multiple brands including McLaren, Ferrari, Lamborghini, and more, AI-driven traffic, and fully dynamic environments. All vehicles can be driven with keyboard, gamepad, or steering wheel controllers. 

Key Features:
- Realistic car physics: acceleration, braking, handling, gears.
- AI traffic system: obeys basic traffic rules and interacts dynamically.
- Premium and regular in-game currency system (Coins and Cash).
- Upgrade system for performance: speed, brakes, handling.
- Coins earned from driving and completing missions.
- Cash as premium currency, convertible from coins (100 coins = 1 cash) or earned in rare achievements.
- Cars are unlockable via coins, cash, or combination, allowing strategic progression.
- Placeholder 3D models, textures, audio, and UI are included.
- Extendable with real assets and logos to reach full AAA polish.
